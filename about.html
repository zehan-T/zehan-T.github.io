Biography艺术家简介

About关于

Chinese soprano Zehan Tian is a candidate for the Doctor of Musical Arts in Opera Performance at the Butler School of Music at The University of Texas at Austin. During the 2026–27 season, she is completing the final year of her doctoral studies and will appear as the Queen of the Night in Mozart’s Die Zauberflöte with the Butler Opera Center. 中国女高音田泽涵，现于德克萨斯大学奥斯汀分校 Butler 音乐学院攻读歌剧表演艺术博士（D.M.A.）。2026–27 演出季是她博士阶段的最后一年，她将与 Butler 歌剧中心合作，出演莫扎特《魔笛》中的夜女王。

In the summer of 2026, Zehan joined the Lyrique-en-Mer Opéra Studio in Belle-Île, France, where she performed Le Feu in Ravel’s L’Enfant et les Sortilèges and appeared in Mozart’s Requiem. Earlier that year, she sang Pamina in Mozart’s Die Zauberflöte with Stafford Opera Troupe. Her other recent appearances at the Butler School of Music include Suor Genovieffa in Puccini’s Suor Angelica, Nella in Gianni Schicchi, and the First Spirit in Massenet’s Cendrillon. She also covered Lauretta in Gianni Schicchi. 2026 年夏，田泽涵加入法国美丽岛的 Lyrique-en-Mer 歌剧工作室，在拉威尔《孩子与魔法》中饰演"火"，并参演莫扎特《安魂曲》。同年稍早，她与 Stafford Opera Troupe 合作演唱莫扎特《魔笛》中的帕米娜。她在 Butler 音乐学院的近期演出还包括普契尼《修女安杰莉卡》中的 Suor Genovieffa、《贾尼·斯基基》中的 Nella，以及马斯内《灰姑娘》中的第一精灵，并担任《贾尼·斯基基》中 Lauretta 的备角。

During the 2024–25 season, Zehan appeared as Barbarina in Mozart’s Le nozze di Figaro and as the Soprano in the Ghost Quartet in John Corigliano’s The Ghosts of Versailles at the Butler School of Music. She also performed Laura Brown in scenes from Kevin Puts’ The Hours and Frasquita in scenes from Bizet’s Carmen with OPERA San Antonio. In 2024, she sang Adele in Johann Strauss II’s Die Fledermaus with Stafford Opera Troupe and covered Annina in Verdi’s La traviata at the Butler School of Music. 2024–25 演出季，她在 Butler 音乐学院饰演莫扎特《费加罗的婚礼》中的芭芭丽娜，以及科里利亚诺《凡尔赛的幽灵》"幽灵四重唱"中的女高音。她还与 OPERA San Antonio 合作，演唱凯文·普茨《时时刻刻》选段中的 Laura Brown 和比才《卡门》选段中的 Frasquita。2024 年，她与 Stafford Opera Troupe 合作演唱小约翰·施特劳斯《蝙蝠》中的阿黛勒，并在 Butler 音乐学院担任威尔第《茶花女》中 Annina 的备角。

Her 2023 engagements included Suor Dolcina in Suor Angelica and covering Susanna in Le nozze di Figaro at Saluzzo Opera Academy in Italy. She also performed Sifare in scenes from Mozart’s Mitridate, re di Ponto at the Accademia Vocale Lorenzo Malfatti and Ilia in scenes from Idomeneo at Indiana University. Her earlier roles and scene work at Xiamen University included Hanna in Lehár’s Die lustige Witwe and Zerlina in Mozart’s Don Giovanni. 2023 年，她在意大利萨卢佐歌剧学院演唱《修女安杰莉卡》中的 Suor Dolcina，并担任《费加罗的婚礼》中苏珊娜的备角。她还在 Accademia Vocale Lorenzo Malfatti 演唱莫扎特《本都国王米特里达特》选段中的 Sifare，并在印第安纳大学演唱《伊多梅纽》选段中的 Ilia。更早在厦门大学期间，她曾演唱雷哈尔《风流寡妇》中的汉娜与莫扎特《唐璜》中的泽琳娜。

Zehan has also appeared in a variety of recital and concert programs. She was featured as a Studio Artist in the Studio Artist Showcase presented by OPERA San Antonio in 2025 and as a Young Artist Singer in the Aria Stage program at Austin Opera Festival in 2024. Her other concert appearances include The Great American Song with Stafford Opera Troupe and Aria Night with the Accademia Vocale Lorenzo Malfatti in Italy. 田泽涵亦活跃于独唱会与音乐会舞台。2025 年，她作为驻团艺术家参加 OPERA San Antonio 的驻团艺术家展演；2024 年，她作为青年艺术家参加奥斯汀歌剧节的 Aria Stage 项目。其他音乐会演出包括与 Stafford Opera Troupe 合作的《The Great American Song》，以及在意大利 Accademia Vocale Lorenzo Malfatti 的 Aria Night。

Her competition honors include Second Prize in the Advanced Performer category at the 2025 International Schubert Competition Vienna and recognition as a Florida Region Finalist in the 2024 Metropolitan Opera Laffont Competition. She was also named the winner of the Vocal Open Group at the 8th Hong Kong International Music Festival. Her earlier awards include the Golden Prize in the Youth Group at the Scriabin International Arts Festival, the Performing Award at the Golden Stars International Music Competition of Valencia, and Second Prize in the Operatic Open Group at the Hong Kong International Vocal Open Competition. 她的比赛荣誉包括 2025 年维也纳国际舒伯特声乐比赛高级演奏组第二名，以及 2024 年大都会歌剧院 Laffont 声乐比赛佛罗里达赛区决赛入围。她还曾获第八届香港国际音乐节声乐公开组第一名。此前的奖项包括斯克里亚宾国际艺术节青年组金奖、瓦伦西亚金星国际音乐比赛表演奖，以及香港国际声乐公开赛歌剧公开组第二名。

Throughout her training, Zehan has worked with stage directors Garnett Bruce, Michael Shell, Benjamin Smith, and Cecilia Stinton, as well as conductors Douglas Kinney Frost, Kevin Class, and Jordon Davidson. She has received additional coaching from Kevin Murphy and Allan Armstrong. She has participated in masterclasses with baritone Lucas Meachem, soprano Heidi Grant Murphy, conductor Francesco Milioto, and soprano Rachel Blaustein. 在学习历程中，田泽涵曾与导演 Garnett Bruce、Michael Shell、Benjamin Smith、Cecilia Stinton，以及指挥 Douglas Kinney Frost、Kevin Class、Jordon Davidson 合作，并得到 Kevin Murphy 与 Allan Armstrong 的艺术指导。她曾参加男中音 Lucas Meachem、女高音 Heidi Grant Murphy、指挥 Francesco Milioto 及女高音 Rachel Blaustein 的大师班。

Originally from China, Zehan holds a Master of Music in Voice Performance from the Indiana University Jacobs School of Music, where she studied voice with Tichina Vaughn and Deanne Meek and coached with Gary Arvin. She earned her Bachelor of Music in Voice Performance from Xiamen University, where she studied with Yalun Zhang. Her additional artistic background includes traditional Chinese dance, ballet, and piano, as well as experience in event management and backstage production. 田泽涵来自中国，获印第安纳大学雅各布斯音乐学院声乐表演硕士学位，师从 Tichina Vaughn 与 Deanne Meek，艺术指导为 Gary Arvin；本科毕业于厦门大学声乐表演专业，师从张亚伦。她的艺术背景还包括中国古典舞、芭蕾与钢琴，并具有活动策划与舞台后台制作经验。
