Introduction简介

01壹

Chinese soprano Zehan Tian is a candidate for the Doctor of Musical Arts in Opera Performance at the Butler School of Music at The University of Texas at Austin. During the 2026–27 season, she is completing the final year of her doctoral studies and will appear as the Queen of the Night in Mozart’s Die Zauberflöte with the Butler Opera Center. 中国女高音田泽涵，现于德克萨斯大学奥斯汀分校 Butler 音乐学院攻读歌剧表演艺术博士（D.M.A.）。2026–27 演出季是她博士阶段的最后一年，她将与 Butler 歌剧中心合作，出演莫扎特《魔笛》中的夜女王。

In the summer of 2026 she joined the Lyrique-en-Mer Opéra Studio in Belle-Île, France, singing Le Feu in Ravel’s L’Enfant et les Sortilèges and appearing in Mozart’s Requiem. Her recent roles include Pamina, Suor Genovieffa, Nella and Barbarina. 2026 年夏，她加入法国美丽岛的 Lyrique-en-Mer 歌剧工作室，演唱拉威尔《孩子与魔法》中的"火"，并参演莫扎特《安魂曲》。近期角色包括帕米娜、Suor Genovieffa、Nella 与芭芭丽娜。

02贰

Ravel’s Fire is a coloratura showpiece — a minute and a half of leaping flame. It was written for exactly the kind of voice that can be both brilliant and light on its feet. 拉威尔笔下的"火"是一段花腔炫技 —— 一分半钟的跳跃火焰，正为既要辉煌、又要轻盈的声音而写。
