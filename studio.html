Teaching教学

Voice Studio声乐工作室

Voice and piano lessons in Austin and online — for beginners, high-school auditionees and college-level singers. 在奥斯汀及线上教授声乐与钢琴 —— 面向初学者、艺考生与音乐院校在读学生。

In the studio教学内容

01壹

Lessons are built around healthy, sustainable technique: breath, registration, resonance and the freedom to sing a long phrase without strain. From there we work outward into repertoire, language and the acting of a song. 课程以健康、可持续的发声技术为核心：呼吸、换声区、共鸣，以及唱完一个长乐句而不感到勒紧的自由。在此基础上，再延伸到曲目、语言与歌曲的表演处理。

Every student already has a voice of their own — my work is to help you find it and make it reliable. Lessons combine technical clarity, musical imagination and guidance shaped around you: classical vocal technique, musicianship, diction, interpretation and audition preparation, paced to your age, experience and goals. 每一位学生都已经拥有属于自己的声音 —— 我要做的，是帮你找到它，并让它稳定可靠。课程融合清晰的技术训练、音乐想象力与因人而异的指导：古典声乐技术、音乐素养、语言正音、作品处理与考试准备，并依据年龄、基础与目标调整节奏。

The technical work is grounded in current pedagogy rather than habit: I hold a Vocalogy certification from the Butler School of Music and completed graduate voice pedagogy coursework at the Indiana University Jacobs School of Music. 技术训练建立在当代教学法之上，而非经验主义：我持有 Butler 音乐学院的 Vocalogy（嗓音学）认证，并在印第安纳大学雅各布斯音乐学院完成研究生声乐教学法课程。

I have taught students from early childhood through adulthood, and I welcome both complete beginners and advanced singers looking to refine technique, artistry and stage confidence. Lessons can be taken in English or Mandarin, and Italian, German and French diction for the standard repertoire is part of the work. 我教过从幼童到成人的各年龄段学生，既欢迎零基础的初学者，也欢迎希望打磨技术、艺术性与舞台自信的进阶歌者。授课语言可选英文或中文，标准曲目所需的意大利语、德语、法语正音亦包含在课程内。
